## Nine Slides

1. **Opening — Agentic Commerce: When AI Enters the Shopping Journey** — Dirk Buursma / Commerce Partners, with Commonwork introduced separately as The Collaborative Commerce Network.
2. **A buying decision before the website visit** — illustrative stroller need adapted from an OpenAI example prompt; no recorded shopping result.
3. **What the shopper can do today** — request, clarification, shortlist, comparison, seller options. Official OpenAI source video is linked, not embedded; it shows/claims shopping research, not autonomous purchase.
4. **Brand visibility is not retailer visibility** — three questions: considered, represented accurately, offered as this seller.
5. **Who shapes the recommendation?** — shopper context, brand data, retailer offer, third-party sources and assistant/platform selection. Influence is not control.
6. **Check your own visibility** — proposed worksheet for a repeatable, contextual observation; no score or benchmark.
7. **From observation to correction** — product/offer facts, source consistency, operational ownership and retest.
8. **Tools for specific jobs** — Observe / Diagnose / Improve / Monitor; not a recommended stack. The Geoffy MP4 is withheld pending content review and reuse permission.
9. **What the retailer does next** — one bounded test, evidence-based prioritisation, resources and the Panel Debate Statements discussion.
