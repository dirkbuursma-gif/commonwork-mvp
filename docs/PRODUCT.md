# Commonwork MVP

## Proposition

Commonwork connects professionals through demonstrated competence, current business needs and trusted personal introductions.

Competence gets someone matched. A purposeful introduction starts the relationship.

## Product principles

1. Competence before identity, title or popularity.
2. Every match must explain its relevance.
3. Personal introductions are preferable to cold outreach.
4. Both parties must consent before an introduction is completed.
5. The platform creates conversations, not recruitment processes.
6. Events activate the network but do not define it.
7. Working groups demonstrate knowledge; they are not social feeds.
8. No age, gender or other demographic attribute may influence matching.
9. Location may only be used as an optional practical constraint.
10. Users control their availability and contact preferences.

## Core loop

1. A member documents competence and evidence.
2. A member or organisation describes competence needed.
3. Commonwork identifies relevant people.
4. Commonwork explains strengths, evidence and gaps.
5. A member requests an introduction.
6. Both parties consent.
7. A mutual contact, Connector or Commonwork completes the introduction.
8. Participants confirm whether the conversation was relevant.

## Explicit exclusions

- Job applications
- Recruitment pipelines
- CV screening
- Follower counts
- Mass messaging
- Public popularity scores
- Demographic matching
- Automated rejection
- Engagement-driven feeds
- Payments
- Native mobile applications
